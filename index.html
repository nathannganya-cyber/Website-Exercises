Eight-week progression ＋
Nutrition & recovery ＋
  • Eat regular, balanced meals. Avoid crash diets and skipping meals.
  • Include protein sources such as eggs, yogurt, beans, tofu, fish, or chicken.
  • Eat a variety of fruits and vegetables, and drink water regularly.
  • Get enough sleep and keep a consistent schedule.
  • Reduce training when unusually tired. If you are still growing, sufficient food and recovery are particularly important.
Progression rules ＋

When all sets reach the upper end of the rep range with good form, slightly increase difficulty. Change one factor at a time. Most sets should end with approximately 1–3 good repetitions still possible.

Examples: wall push-ups → incline push-ups → knee or regular push-ups; regular squats → slower controlled squats; short planks → longer holds with good alignment.
