THE PROGRAM

Eight weeks. One routine.

Goal: improve strength, conditioning, consistency, and support your goal of getting leaner.

Training structure

3 strength sessions · 2 cardio sessions · 1 recovery day · 1 full rest day each week.

Session duration

Strength: 25–35 minutes. Cardio: 20–30 minutes. Recovery: 5–10 minutes.

Rest between sets

45–90 seconds depending on the exercise. Rest longer if you need to maintain good form.

Equipment

Bodyweight only. Use a sturdy, non-slip counter for incline push-ups if appropriate.

Weekly progression

Nutrition and recovery

How to measure progress

  • Track push-ups completed with good technique.
  • Notice squat and lunge control.
  • Track comfortable plank duration.
  • Notice how easily cardio sessions feel.
  • Monitor energy, recovery, and consistency.

Review every two weeks. Body weight fluctuates and is not the only measure of progress.
